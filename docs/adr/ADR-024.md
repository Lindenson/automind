# ADR-024: Open-world vehicle identity (make registry plus sourced line-ups), identified on every intake turn

**Status**: Accepted · 2026-11-03 (spec 005 FR-001–FR-015; research R1–R5, R10)

## Context

In chat 5be54966 the hub said "I don't know a car called «byd»" four times in a row. A make or model outside the catalog and `generations.yaml` was rejected unless the resolver gave a generation. The status `UNKNOWN_MODEL` stuck to the car, and a correct answer that repeated the same make and model was treated as "not understood". The engine was not part of the identity, so "1.5, not 1.0" changed nothing.

## Decision

- **An `identify` step after `apply_delta`.** For every unsettled or changed car it runs the identification pipeline again from the current facts. Each outcome is recorded on the car: IDENTIFIED, CHOICES, NEED or NOT_FOUND.
- **Make registry: `config/makes.yaml`.** About 60 makes sold in Spain, with aliases, Russian transliterations and the official Spanish sites. Matching is deterministic: aliases, then the URL domain, then a fuzzy match. Models are matched on the transliterated text and a consonant skeleton.
- **Brand line-ups: `application/lineup.py`, table `brand_lineups`.**
  - Found by one Spanish search plus the official site, and read through the page store (robots.txt respected, the deny-list never read).
  - Extracted by the `lineup` web reader (no tools).
  - A model is kept only if it is written in its document. A price is kept only if it is in its excerpt.
  - Stored only with at least one official source or one source that states a price. Cached for 30 days.
  - A model in the line-up is known even when the resolver gives no generation. "Unknown" is said only after the line-up lookup.
- **The engine is part of the identity.** `engine_hint` holds displacement, power, fuel and family. A deterministic parser handles negations ("not 1.0 but 1.5"), and the interpreter output is corrected by code. `vehicle_variants` gains `engine_cc`, `power_kw` and `fuel`.
- **Corrections.** Any identity change drops the resolved version. The old label is kept for the "before → after" notice, and the car is identified again.
- **Bounded attempts.** Two attempts per item, then a proposal; four per car, then "remove it or let me choose". A repeated question uses other words.

## Consequences

New brands work without catalog edits, and every name offered to the user has a source. Identification can cost one line-up lookup per make per month, plus the resolver calls it needed before. Research jobs of a version the user corrected away from are skipped.
