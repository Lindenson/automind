# ADR-023: Declarative per-profile sections, one data panel, parallel page pipelines

**Status**: Accepted · 2026-11-03 (spec 004 FR-002, FR-014, FR-019, FR-023, FR-032)

## Decision

Report blocks are tagged with a section, open-by-default and depth from `config/report_layouts.yaml`. The UI renders them as collapsible parts, and the writer receives the depth per section. Gaps appear in one `data_panel`, with "find it" for each car and area, instead of a warning per section. The panel sends `find{focus_cars, areas}`, which researches only those areas and needs no interpretation call.

Inside a research job, every page runs its own concurrent pipeline: relevance, then reading or extraction, then validation, then storage. Concurrency limits apply per job and across the gateway (`config/search.yaml`). Progress is published as `progress` events and shown as a car × area grid.

Depreciation and manufacturer are context areas. They are researched alongside a car's other research or in DEEP research, never on their own for a cached car.

## Consequences

The persona now changes what is explained and how deeply, not only the order of blocks. A cached report never triggers unexpected research costs.
