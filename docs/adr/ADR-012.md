# ADR-012: Versioned research artifacts and models

**Status**: Accepted · 2026-11-02

## Context

Results must be reproducible (constitution IX).

## Decision

Versions recorded: criteria dictionary (`criteria_version`), TCO rules (`tco-1.0`), race model, evidence rules, prompts (header comments). TCO estimates and simulations are stored per turn with their inputs.

## Consequences

A past answer can be traced to the rules that produced it.
