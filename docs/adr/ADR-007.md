# ADR-007: Human in the loop through interrupts

**Status**: Accepted · 2026-11-02

## Context

Variants must be confirmed when ambiguous, used or new to the catalog; expensive research needs consent (FR-006, FR-041, constitution VI).

## Decision

Confirmation nodes are split into prepare → `interrupt` → apply, so side effects never repeat when LangGraph re-runs a node on resume. A free-text reply resumes the interrupt and is interpreted (it may be a correction).

## Consequences

Pending confirmations survive restarts (persistent thread per chat).
