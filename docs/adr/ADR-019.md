# ADR-019: Feasibility engine and the used-price estimate

**Status**: Accepted · 2026-11-02

## Context

Reports were written for requests that could not fit the user's budget or needs. Secondary-market listing prices
are out of scope (classifieds are deny-listed).

## Decision

Budget (with new/used intent, currency, monthly), running-cost limit and usage needs are part of the case file;
numbers must be written in the user's message. Pure rules give a verdict per car and check before the report
(`FEASIBILITY` action with report / used / alternatives). Used age ranges come from editorial price ranges
(`used_price` research area) when found, else from a configured depreciation curve labelled ASSUMPTION; both are
shown when they disagree, the editorial range decides (clarification Q1 = C).

## Consequences

Depreciation curves and thresholds are data the operator must review. The verdict is persisted in
`feasibility_checks` and stays visible in the report.
