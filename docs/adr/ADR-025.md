# ADR-025: One intake confirmation card instead of separate variant and cost confirmations

**Status**: Accepted · 2026-11-03 (spec 005 FR-016–FR-024; research R6–R9, R11)

## Decision

Before research the hub shows one card (`CONFIRM_INTAKE`, `application/intake_card.py`, `IntakeCard.tsx`). It contains:
- per car: make, model, generation, engine, fuel, gearbox, new or used (year and mileage if used), version and the official price;
- the person: use, km a year as a range, region with its climate, budget, priorities;
- the research cost.

Every item carries its basis:
- STATED: the user's words;
- INFERRED: with the evidence words;
- LOOKED_UP: with a source;
- ASSUMED: for example "new" when nobody said otherwise.

Chips edit an item through `intake_edit`. The edit is applied by code, the car is identified again, and the card is shown again with the change highlighted. Free text on the card counts as a correction; a plain "yes" or "no" counts only when it is a bare answer. One "yes" confirms the cars and approves the cost. The hard cap still applies.

The card appears in three cases:
- a proposed version waits for the user;
- questions were asked during the intake;
- the estimate is at or above the confirmation threshold.

A simple, unambiguous comparison of cached or catalogued cars runs as before (constitution VI allows research below the threshold when the identity is not in doubt). The old CONFIRM_VARIANTS and CONFIRM_COST dialogs remain only for chats that were waiting on one before 005.

Questions come from `config/questions.yaml` v2 in en, ru and es, each with `bare`, `rephrase` and `rephrase_bare` variants, so there is never an empty "did you mean". The asker model writes only one sentence of acknowledgement, and a sentence in another language is dropped. While an intake step is pending, a reply is never routed to the `general` role: a brand explanation sets the make. New or used is asked once for every car, and only together with an identity question.

## Consequences

One round trip less before research, and the user sees exactly what will be researched. The golden dialogues G1–G3 and G7 now expect `confirm_kind: intake`.
