# ADR-028: Energy model per powertrain, market facts with dates, like-for-like ownership cost

**Status**: Accepted · 2026-11-04 (spec 006, PRs #28–#36)

## Context

In live chat 5be54966, the only consumption found for the BYD Seal U DM-i was the weighted WLTP figure,
0.9 l/100 km. It was rejected as a unit error, so the BYD's ownership cost had no energy line at all, while the
SEAT Ateca had €2,580 a year of petrol. The old `rates.yaml` also applied a PHEV factor of 1.8 to the weighted
figure (0.9 × 1.8 = 1.6 l/100 km), which makes no physical sense. Batteries, ranges, charging and the user's
charging situation were not modelled, and neither were incentives or the resale value of new brands.

## Decision

1. **One deterministic energy model per powertrain** (`domain/energy.py`):
   - ICE, MHEV and HEV keep the fuel figure. The real-world value is preferred, else WLTP × factor.
   - PHEV and EREV split the year into an electric and a fuel part:
     - share `s = h·min(d, R)/d`, capped at 0.95;
     - `d` is the daily km, or annual km ÷ 300;
     - `R` is the real electric range, WLTP ÷ (1.2 × climate × motorway);
     - `h` is 0.9 with home or work charging and 0.25 without.

     The electric part is priced from kWh/100 km and the fuel part from the consumption with an empty battery
     × 1.15. **The weighted WLTP figure is shown but never priced.**
   - BEV uses a real test first, else WLTP × 1.2. Charging losses (AC 10 %, DC 5 %) are added only to
     battery-side figures, because EU WLTP is measured at the plug.
   - A missing input gives "cannot be computed" with the missing inputs named. In that case the cost does not
     decide the winner, and the specs area is not met, so a deeper round looks for the input first.
2. **Meaning rules for EV values** (`domain/sense_ev.py`):
   - The words around a value decide what it is: weighted, charge-sustaining, electric, gross or usable.
   - kWh is energy and kW is power.
   - DC charging time counts only for a 10–80 % window.
   - Pair checks reject a range the battery cannot give, a DC peak above 5 C, and a usable capacity above the
     gross one.
   - The brand rules accept a dealer count only next to a network word, and a warranty only with plausible
     years.
3. **Prices are dated, sourced assumptions** (`config/energy.yaml`):
   - home PVPC, fixed and night tariffs;
   - public AC and DC charging;
   - petrol and diesel.

   Each price has `as_of`, a source and a basis, and a stale price is labelled. The user's own price, a night
   tariff or free charging at work override the defaults. The home-charging question is asked once, and only
   when the answer would change the winner.
4. **One efficiency scale.** All powertrains are compared on € per 100 km at the user's prices and charging
   mix. When one car has no estimate, efficiency is left out for all of them.
5. **Market facts.** Incentives are facts about a market, not a car:
   - They are stored in `market_facts` (migration 0011) behind the `market_facts` port.
   - They are researched once per market in the area `incentives`, which stays fresh for 30 days, has no car
     relevance gate, and checks official sites first.
   - Each fact needs a verbatim excerpt that holds its amount.
   - The status is recomputed from the dates on read. Only an active purchase subsidy with an amount (national,
     or in the user's region) reduces the break-even price difference, and the sentence names it.
6. **Like-for-like ownership cost.**
   - A make that is new in Spain (`makes.yaml: new_in_market`) and has no sourced resale value gets
     "insufficient data for this brand" instead of a class curve.
   - When depreciation is known for some cars and not for others, the period totals leave it out for every car.
7. **The DGT label is derived** from the published rule, shown with that rule and its source.
8. **Measurement.** Each report writes one `ENERGY_REPORT` intake event: cars without an energy line, EV values
   rejected per rule, and weighted figures kept out of the cost (FR-022).

## Consequences

- The incident pair now has an energy line for both cars (golden G8). The goldens G9–G12 cover a BEV against a
  diesel, two BEVs, an HEV against a PHEV, and a used BEV.
- Values stored before these rules keep their old labels until the data repair runs (`scripts/repair-data.sh`,
  with a dry run first and the owner's yes). Before the repair, a stored 0.9 l on a PHEV stays a rejected
  consumption, so the energy line says "cannot be computed" instead of using it.
- The incentives cost one market-level research job a month. Facts without dates stay "dates unclear" and never
  count.
