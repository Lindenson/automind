# ADR-022: Sense layer for values; gearbox, drive and equipment level in the variant identity

**Status**: Accepted · 2026-11-03 (spec 004 FR-027–FR-031; user reports of 2026-11-02)

## Decision

`domain/sense.py` (rules in `config/sense.yaml`) checks that a value means what its attribute means, in addition to the excerpt, unit and plausibility checks:
- consumption is kept per test cycle: WLTP, the older NEDC, and real-world;
- WLTP ranges give their upper bound;
- service intervals in thousands, or in years read as months, are corrected;
- e-CVT and BEV drives have no gear count;
- a value of another gearbox or drive belongs to another variant;
- prices are taken from Spanish sources only and service schedules from European ones;
- a yearly ownership cost outside the configured range is flagged and not scored.

Excerpt checks ignore markdown markup.

Variants carry `transmission`, `drive`, `trim`, `trim_level` and `price_eur`. Cars are compared at one equipment level, the base version by default. Identification is a loop: an answer that does not settle a car is asked again, and no research starts before every car is settled.

`scripts/repair-data.sh` re-checks stored data whenever these rules change.

## Consequences

Older data can lose values that were wrongly accepted, for example the Prado's NEDC 7.4 l/100 km, which no longer counts as WLTP. The fuel bill uses real-world consumption where it is known, otherwise WLTP × a labelled allowance.
